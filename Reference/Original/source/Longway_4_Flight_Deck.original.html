What this is — and is not

An original expandable meshless exploration game, not the other developer’s secret code or an Unreal-equivalent engine. World geometry is traced from mathematical height fields; gas bodies and rings use analytic intersections. One screen-filling triangle runs the pixel shader. Decorative sky stars are not all navigable objects. Navigation lists the generated destinations. Gas giant surface ports are floating habitats above the cloud envelope, not solid ground.

10,000 seeded planet descriptors combine 14 ecological families. Only the nearby landscape and animal populations are active; not 10,000 entire biospheres every frame. Settlements are explorable exteriors. Stations have open hangars, collision, docking and trading. Water shading/buoyancy, lava damage and gravity are game approximations; not fluid dynamics or a scientific ecology model. No specific FPS is guaranteed.
