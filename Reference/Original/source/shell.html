Across the frontier

Use Destinations or the Galaxy atlas to choose a surface port, wilderness site, orbit or station. After landing, press F to exit your ship. Return to the boarding ramp and press F again to take the flight deck. Gas giants have floating habitats and orbital stations.

Open Tactical for an orbital or surface encounter. In Crew Link, host a session and give your six-character code to the other pilots. The host starts shared encounters. Return to a port and use Tactical to rearm and repair.
