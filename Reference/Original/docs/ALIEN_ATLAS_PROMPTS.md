# Alien life atlas prompts

Generated using the built-in image_gen tool. Two original square 4 × 4 albedo atlases; runtime code extracts cells and derives approximate normal/AO/roughness maps. These derived maps are artistic, not material scans.

## Flora

Use case: stylized-concept, production game albedo texture atlas for Space Patriot. Generate ONE square image containing an exact 4 by 4 grid of sixteen equally sized edge-to-edge square material swatches, with absolutely no gutters, borders, text, labels, numbers, perspective, objects, or cast shadows. This is a usable texture asset, not a presentation board. Each cell must be a continuous close-up surface pattern, naturalistic high detail with restrained selective faceting, photorealistic plant tissue and veins, flat diffuse illumination, no baked specular highlights. Rows from top left: row 1 deep violet waxy leaf lamina with branching plum veins; indigo blue narrow grass blade tissue; dark plum bark with longitudinal fibrous ridges; burgundy fern leaflet surface. Row 2 charcoal violet protective bark with fine fissures; blue-black waxy cuticle with subtle cellular scales; muted copper purple thermoregulatory leaf veins; moss green and violet lichen granular surface. Row 3 silvery blue frost-tolerant plant tissue; pale lilac densely felted leaf surface; dark indigo reed stem vertical fibres; violet segmented palm bark. Row 4 aubergine ribbed succulent skin; teal-violet low growing fern tissue; deep purple leaf surface with restrained amber speckling; navy blue soft grass tissue. Rich but subdued alien botanical palette, clear differences between cells, biologically coherent microstructure, texture fully fills every cell, no background. Orthographic scan, crisp consistent scale across all sixteen surfaces. Do not put an entire leaf or plant on a background.

## Fauna

Use case: stylized-concept, production game albedo texture atlas for Space Patriot alien wildlife. ONE square image: exact 4 by 4 grid of sixteen equal square, edge-to-edge material swatches. No text, labels, gutters, borders, whole animals, eyes, limbs, silhouettes, perspective, shadows, glare, or backgrounds. Continuous naturalistic high detail skin/fur/feather material close-ups fill each cell. Flat diffuse scanned-surface illumination suitable for actual PBR mesh texturing, restrained selective faceting and organic anatomy, subtle alien variation. Row 1: muted plum fine mammalian fur; blue-grey pebbled reptile scales; aubergine iridescent feather barbs with subtle copper edges; dark violet tightly joined beetle chitin scales. Row 2: charcoal protective keratin plate surface; dark indigo dense short fur; copper mauve fine ridged cooling-fin skin; slate blue smooth reptile hide with tiny cream flecks. Row 3: silver lilac insulating fur; dark lavender down feathers; dusty violet scale mosaic with thin branching markings; charcoal-blue chitin with plum seams. Row 4: muted rust-brown antler keratin fibres; violet granular skin with very sparse tiny turquoise bioluminescent freckles, restrained and not bright; midnight blue and ochre fine mottled hide; lavender-grey small reptile scales. Avoid blood, injuries, armor machinery or cartoon designs. Biological surface microstructures, subtle realistic variation, crisp enough for natural proportioned animated game animals. All sixteen cells differ clearly while belonging to a coherent grounded science-fiction world.
